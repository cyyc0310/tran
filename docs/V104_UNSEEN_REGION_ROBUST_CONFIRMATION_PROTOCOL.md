# V104 protocol: frozen factor-robust sharp-set confirmation on unseen regions

Frozen 2026-10-28 before scoring. Test CPLE, FPC, IPCO, PNM, and SOCO in Jan–Jun 2026. The roster is formed from the V52 quality-only census: these regions were excluded from its all-period cohort, each has at least 0.98 complete-proxy coverage in 2026, and exact-code searches of prior `docs/` and `scripts/` found no prior region-specific analysis. Do not rank or replace regions using CIF outputs. The V52 census itself contains coverage and schema information only.

Apply the V103 method without changes: monthly fuel-energy margins, 2025 EIA-860 technology-group capacities, exact sharp K sets at nominal factors and at coordinated −10%/+10% thermal-factor perturbations, for thresholds 600/900 gCO2/kWh and required event rates 1/5/10%. Retain all months with at least 80% complete proxy-label hours and group-energy capacity feasibility; do not filter on observed hourly peaks. For unavailable months, report abstention. The robust result is the endpoint hull `[min K_lowEF, max K_highEF]`; it covers every factor vector in the box by monotonicity, but does not claim every integer in the hull is jointly feasible under one global factor vector.

Primary summaries are macro averages over eligible region-months with equal region weighting, alongside pooled summaries and per-region results. Report denominators, unavailable months, capacity compatibility, observed proxy K containment, and the V52 coverage audit. This is a blind transfer across regions in an already observed time period, not a prospective time holdout. EIA fixed-factor emissions are proxy labels, the factor box is a stress scenario rather than an estimated uncertainty interval, and monthly totals are target-month information; therefore the task is retrospective identification/nowcast, not month-ahead forecasting or validation of actual CIF.
