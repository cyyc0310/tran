# V140 — Literature Boundary for the Monthly-Information Experiments

Primary sources checked on 2026-10-28:

| Prior work | Established contribution relevant here | Consequence for TransCIF |
|---|---|---|
| [Romano, Patterson & Candès, Conformalized Quantile Regression](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.03222) | Combines quantile regression and conformal calibration to adapt interval width to heteroscedasticity. | Same-budget CQR is a required baseline. Conditional intervals or nonconstant widths are not new contributions. |
| [Liu, de Paula & Tamer, Prediction Sets and Conformal Inference with Interval Outcomes, revised February 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.10117) | Studies prediction with censored/interval outcomes, partial identification and conformal sets. | Treating EF-box K as an interval-valued target and conformalizing its endpoints is insufficient novelty by itself. Their oracle target/coverage definition must be compared in full before claiming theoretical equivalence or difference. |
| [Tibshirani et al., Conformal Prediction Under Covariate Shift](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.06019) | Weighted conformal prediction addresses covariate shift under likelihood-ratio information and associated assumptions. | Adding target-feature weights or donor similarity is not automatically novel, and it cannot justify arbitrary changes in hourly scheduling conditional on the monthly inputs. |
| [Wen, Ahmad & Schniter, Task-Driven Uncertainty Quantification in Inverse Problems via Conformal Prediction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12109201/) | Quantifies uncertainty of a downstream task from ill-posed inverse reconstruction and considers additional measurements. | A downstream risk target and a minimum-information experiment alone are broad ideas with prior art. The energy-specific identified set, assumptions and information contracts need a concrete technical increment. |

These are an original-source screening check, not a completed systematic novelty review. V139's physical normalization is a useful controlled baseline. V140 tests whether learning the normalized feasible position adds anything beyond direct CQR and an active-month control. A positive result would motivate a deeper dependence/transfer mechanism; it would not establish originality merely by naming a new score.

The scientific bottleneck is explicit: monthly fuel margins constrain fuel totals but leave intra-month co-occurrence and dispatch underdetermined. Source learning can reduce empirical uncertainty only under a transfer assumption about that hidden structure. A credible paper must identify that assumption, diagnose its support using available information, compare strong baselines, and retain uncertainty when the assumption is unsupported. Target-month reconstruction, future prediction of monthly inputs, and hourly prediction accuracy must be reported as separate evaluation objects.
