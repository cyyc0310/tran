# V117 — Q3-Matched Group-Conformal Diagnostic Results

Season-matched calibration used the 25 source regions' July–September 2025 monthly scores, aggregated by region maximum, with split-conformal rank 24 at nominal 90% coverage. Radii dropped from V116's all-month values of 0.5282/0.1097 to 0.2782/0.00134 for τ=600/900.

On V115's 11 eligible 2026Q3 region-months, the intersection narrowed to 51.2 h at τ=600 and 0.17 h at τ=900. However, τ=600 factor-box proxy endpoint coverage fell to 10/11: TEPC September's upper endpoint was 232 h, while the intersection stopped at 200 h. τ=900 coverage was 11/11 and the negative-certificate rate rose to 100%, but all 11 observed factor-box counts were zero, so this is an easy zero-support case rather than evidence of useful high-risk discrimination.

This seasonal calibrator is rejected as a general replacement: the extreme τ=900 narrowing is not informative on the selected zero-event cohort, and it provides too little support for the nonzero TEPC τ=600 case.
