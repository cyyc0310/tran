# Local thermal-fuel information value evidence

Paired nine-country strict-CN5 leave-one-country-out development experiments. The two additional information arms use revised Energy-Charts fuel generation aggregates and are not current Chinese NBS inputs.

- `annual_macro_metrics.csv`, `annual_country_metrics.csv`, `annual_bandwidth_selection.csv`: M vs previous-year local annual thermal fuel EF.
- `monthly_macro_metrics.csv`, `monthly_country_metrics.csv`, `monthly_calibration_radii.csv`: M vs target `t−2` local monthly thermal fuel EF, with all three arms.
- Full sealed hourly predictions and complete manifests are under `results/cn5-lagged-local-fuel-mix-information-r1/` and `results/cn5-lagged-monthly-fuel-mix-information-r1/`.

See [combined results](../../CN5_LOCAL_THERMAL_FUEL_INFO_VALUE_RESULTS_20261101.md), the [annual protocol](../../CN5_LAGGED_LOCAL_FUEL_MIX_INFORMATION_PROTOCOL_20261101.md), and the [monthly protocol](../../CN5_LAGGED_MONTHLY_FUEL_MIX_INFORMATION_PROTOCOL_20261101.md).
