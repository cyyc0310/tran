# V105 protocol: paired monthly-margin versus coarse-capacity ablation

Frozen 2026-10-28. Reuse exactly the five V104 regions, six 2026H1 months, monthly label-coverage rule, EIA-860 2025 capacity vintage, thresholds, event rates, and nominal/±10% thermal-factor stress box. Do not change the V104 roster or month eligibility.

Compare (A) sharp monthly fuel-margin K set without capacity and (B) exact monthly-margin plus shared thermal/nuclear/hydro/wind/solar group-capacity K set. For each information condition, calculate low, nominal, and high factor sets and report the monotone endpoint hull. Verify the fixed-factor proxy K lies in both hulls and that (B) is contained in (A); any failure is a protocol error. Report paired widths and positive/negative/abstention decisions at region-month, per-region, equal-region macro, and pooled levels. Unavailable V104 months remain unavailable; do not treat them as successful abstentions or silently drop them from a deployment denominator.

This is a post-V104 mechanistic ablation on a fixed unseen-region roster, not a new blind test. The labels remain fixed-factor EIA generation proxies, the ±10% factor box is a scenario, and month aggregates are target-month inputs. Results therefore concern partial identification/retrospective reconstruction, not calibrated forecasts or actual Chinese CIF.
