# V106 protocol: unseen-region China-style coarse monthly information budget

Frozen 2026-10-28. Reuse the V104 quality-selected regions CPLE/FPC/IPCO/PNM/SOCO, Jan–Jun 2026, all 30 V104-eligible months, 2025 capacity vintage, and thresholds 600/900 gCO₂/kWh with 1/5/10% event-rate targets. No region or month may be added or dropped after looking at K results.

Hide all within-thermal monthly fuel margins. Retain only monthly thermal, nuclear, hydro, wind, and solar totals. For thermal, allow the event and complement hours to have independently varying factors in the proxy support [400,1000] gCO₂/kWh; nonthermal factors are zero as in the frozen EIA proxy. Derive event and complement generation intervals from each group's month total and (a) no capacity constraint, or (b) the lagged annual group capacity. Enumerate the vertices of the event/complement feasibility polygon to obtain the K set. The no-capacity set must contain the capacity set; both must contain observed fixed-factor proxy K. Count energy-incompatible groups/months as unavailable, preserving the V104 denominator.

Compare selective positive/negative/abstention frontiers and widths to V105's detailed-fuel ±10% EF-box capacity set. The two factor regimes are intentionally distinct: this experiment asks what is possible under a coarse public-field contract, while V105 uses detailed local fuel margins. This is a post-hoc heldout-region information-budget audit, not a fresh blind test or prospective forecast. Labels are fixed-factor EIA proxies; group-month margins are target-month inputs; annual capacity is a one-year lagged U.S. analogue, not verified Chinese capacity data.
