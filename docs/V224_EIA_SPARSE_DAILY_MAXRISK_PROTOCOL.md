# V224 EIA sparse-day calibration for hourly CIF risk

Date: 2026-10-30. Final run: r3. Earlier r1/r2 artifacts are retired; r2 used an incorrect timestamp unit in the daily coverage aggregation. Only r3 results are valid.

## Question

Can a few randomly sampled local days of full hourly fuel generation calibrate the hourly-risk radius around the known monthly generation-weighted anchor? Unlike pointwise hourly conformal calibration, each calibration unit is a **whole day**, scored by the maximum hourly absolute error that day. This avoids treating the 24 correlated hourly values as 24 independent calibration examples.

## Data and split

The runner consumes the enriched hourly cache generated by the V219 preparation workflow at `/tmp/v219_eia_hourly_series.pkl`; V219 documents the raw EIA source files and hashes.

- Native EIA-930 balance-area hourly fuel generation source files cached by V219.
- 2025 provides source calibration profiles; 2026 January–June is the target period.
- For each target region, all its history is excluded from the source calibration pool. The source pool uses other eligible regions and the matching calendar month.
- 24 regions and 135 region-months satisfy the existing data completeness criteria.
- The label is hourly CIF mechanically calculated from EIA hourly fuel generation with fixed emission factors. It is not independently metered emissions.
- The center is fixed to each target month's generation-weighted fixed-factor mean. It is known only post-month.

## Local information budget

A sampled local day supplies its full 24-hour, eight-fuel generation curve. Its calibration score is the maximum absolute hourly deviation from the unchanged monthly center. Test scoring uses all hours in unsampled days. Budgets are 3, 5, 7, 9, 12, and 15 random days, with 100 random partitions per region-month-budget.

The 90% conformal radius uses rank `ceil(0.9*(n+1))`; 9 is the first finite-radius budget. We report both per-hour marginal coverage and simultaneous all-hours-within-day coverage. The latter has a day as the evaluation unit. Empirical coverage over repeated random partitions does not promise coverage for a fixed sample, every future day, or every region.

## Baseline and uncertainty

The source-only baseline is calibrated on 2025 daily-maximum scores from other regions, matching the target calendar month. Paired confidence intervals resample the 24 regions. Daily sampling is without replacement. Results do not establish direct CIF accuracy and inherit EIA fixed-factor proxy limitations.
