# V352 protocol: threshold robustness of mix × capacity complementarity

Extend the V350/V351 2×2 exact K-set factorial to τ=100, 300, and 500 g/kWh, on identical 328 province-month keys. Hold the fuel-share stress band at ±100%, and retain the matched [0,1100] aggregate thermal EF support. Compare fuel-resolved monthly effective-EF support against its collapsed five-technology outer support, crossed with no effective hourly capacity bound and lagged 2022 CEC group capacities.

Report each threshold's paired width and difference-in-differences interaction. This tests whether the V351 interaction is specific to τ=300 or persists across lower and higher risk thresholds. It remains conditional on Ember-estimated generation and analyst-selected share errors; it is not hourly forecast validation or interval-coverage evidence.
